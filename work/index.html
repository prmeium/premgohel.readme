THE BRIEF

Find potential design partners for an AI execution platform serving teams with complex operations. The first task was to understand where coordination actually broke down.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I researched fashion brands and manufacturers across Indian cities, mapped founders and functional leaders, made outreach calls, documented discovery, and created brand-specific one-pagers and follow-up material.

A consolidated prospect pipeline, actionable discovery notes, and tailored sales collateral. This was prospecting and discovery work; it is not presented as closed customer revenue.

THE BRIEF

Introduce students to useful AI study workflows and create a reason to try the product beyond using AI as a search box.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I prepared and delivered the demonstration, using a topic explainer and flashcards generated from study material as concrete entry points.

THE CONSTRAINT

The session had a projector but no student devices. The school also limited direct commercial promotion, so the demonstration had to carry the value.

OBSERVED RESULT

Approximately 70 students attended and approximately 15 downloads were reported afterward. These are session-level observations; retention and paid conversion were not established.
